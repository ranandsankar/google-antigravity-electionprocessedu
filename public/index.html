The Indian Election Journey

Understand the step-by-step process of how elections work in India.

📝

1. Voter Registration

Ensure your name is on the Electoral Roll. First-time voters use Form 6 to register and get their EPIC (Voter ID).

📅

2. Election Schedule & MCC

The ECI announces the schedule. The Model Code of Conduct (MCC) immediately comes into effect to ensure fair campaigning.

📄

3. Before Voting

Check your polling station online. Political parties may issue a Voter Information Slip, but an official ID is required to vote.

🚶

4. Polling Booth Steps

At the booth, officials verify your identity, apply indelible ink to your finger, and authorize you to cast your vote.

🔘

5. Casting Vote (EVM & VVPAT)

Press the button next to your choice on the Electronic Voting Machine (EVM) or choose NOTA. A VVPAT slip confirms your choice.

🔢

6. Counting

EVMs are securely transported and counted in the presence of candidates under strict ECI supervision.

✅

7. Results Declaration

The Returning Officer officially declares the result, and the Election Commission formalizes the new assembly.
